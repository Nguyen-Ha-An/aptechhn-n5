# FocusLearn direction and reasons

Reading: a study desk for students, with a quiet notebook visual language.
Dial: ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1.

- Warm paper and ink support long reading; terracotta identifies actions.
- Georgia headings give notebook character; system sans keeps forms readable.
- A single column follows the task, note, focus sequence with no hidden screens.
- Ruled section headings repeat the notebook motif and separate content.
- Generous section gaps separate activities; rows stay compact for scanning.
- No cards, icons, images, shadows or animation are needed for these features.
- Light/dark choice supports different reading conditions; neither is forced.
- Example tasks and notes are identified once in the footer, not in the brand.
